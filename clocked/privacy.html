Clocked Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Clocked is a pre-med hours log. It is built so your information stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (experiences, hours, dates, contacts, descriptions and shift notes) is stored only on your device. Clocked has no accounts and no servers, and we cannot see or access this information. If your phone is backed up to iCloud, Apple may include this data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple; it does not receive anything you type into Clocked. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

Shift reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Exports

When you export a PDF or spreadsheet, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

What we don't do

Clocked has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share personal information.

Patient privacy

Please don't enter patient names or any details that could identify a patient in your notes.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete all data in the app, or delete the app from your phone.

Children

Clocked is intended for college students and adults and is not directed to children under 13.
