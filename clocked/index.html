Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your phone. Clocked has no accounts or servers.

I got a new phone. How do I get Premium back? Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Clocked.

How do I export my hours? Settings › Export › PDF report or Spreadsheet (Premium).
